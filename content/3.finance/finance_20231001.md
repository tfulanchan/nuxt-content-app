question: "Fred David invested in the stock of a hypothetical company called Stars Ltd. He purchased three shares worth USD100 each at the beginning of the first year. He invested in another share worth USD115 before the beginning of the second year. He sold the four shares at the end of the second year for a price of USD120 per share. At the end of each period, the stock paid a dividend of USD2 per share. Which of the following is most likely to be the money-weighted rate of return?"

answers: [

Fred also received dividends worth USD6 on the shares purchased earlier and thus an inflow of USD6.         Net cash outflow of USD109    At t = 3, Fred sold 4 shares for worth USD120 each therefore an inflow of USD480. Step 2:   Given the following Net Cash flows, calculate the IRR which is equivalent to the money-weighted return.      CF0 = -300, CF1 = -109 and CF2 = 488. IRR or money weighted rate of return = 10.66%."
